Final pass: cancelled-Match Round completion
---------------------------------------------
A Match with status "cancelled" is now terminal only for Round-completion
purposes. A Round may transition after every scheduled Match is either
"completed" or "cancelled". Cancelled Matches remain excluded from winner,
standings, ratings, Swiss points, and Match-completion projections. Existing
submitted-result evidence is retained only as an audit record if a live Match
is cancelled while awaiting validation; it is not treated as a completed result.
The cancellation lifecycle now sends the normal post-commit Match state-change
notification and invokes only the existing authoritative Round transition when
the cancelled Match belongs to a Round.

Result-dependent Super Mêlée pairing remains fail-closed when a cancelled Match
has no winner or loser. The system does not invent a winner, score, or pairing
in that case.
